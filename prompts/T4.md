# Comparing three job queues (independent blog, March 2026)
In our tests on commodity hardware QuickQueue sustained about 90,000 jobs per second, and only
after tuning its batch size. Sidekiq-style Redis queues reached 60,000; a PostgreSQL-backed
queue (SKIP LOCKED) reached 12,000 but needed no extra infrastructure. For teams under ten
people we recommend starting with the database you already run.

=== incident-postmortem.md ===
# Postmortem: lost jobs after queue failover (internal, 2025-11)
During a node failover our QuickQueue cluster acknowledged 3,400 jobs that were never executed.
Root cause: acknowledgement before persistence in the default configuration. Fix: enable
durable-ack mode, which reduced throughput by about 40 %. Lesson: test failover before trusting
throughput numbers.
